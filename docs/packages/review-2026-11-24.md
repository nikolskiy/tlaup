### Findings

### E1. Blocker. The checkpoint fake hangs and never reaches 2196 (01 T4, T5; 11 R6 Tests; 20 G3 Tests)

T4's `checkpoint` row, as revised for Q10, says: "With the 2195 line at `n` and the 2196 line at `m` in the replayed text, and `hang_after_line` between them, this is the checkpoint the deferral scenarios of R6 and G3 need." T4 defines `hang_after_line` as "stop after a line and wait for a signal". T5's new note says "the fake hangs there until 2196".

In the one-signal deferral, the fake never gets that signal:

- Under D60, a parsed run's JVM is in its own process group, so the SIGINT reaches tlaup alone.
- tlaup holds that SIGINT until it reads 2196 (G3: "the grace period starts at 2196, not at the signal").
- The fake does not write 2196 until a signal arrives.

So the scenarios R6, G3 and T5 describe cannot finish. They are R6's "a SIGINT sent `once java has begun its checkpoint` ... the fake gets its SIGTERM only after 2196, and the folder is kept" and G3's pseudo-terminal scenario. Both wait until they time out.

The packages copy the fault:

- 03's `test_fakes.py` asks that "without the signal the `.chkpt` files exist by the time line `m` can be read", with `hang_after_line` between `n` and `m`.
- 39's scenario uses "`java hangs after line <n>` between them as T4 has it".

The signed-off T4 had the same phrase. The working-in rewrote this row and these steps for Q10 and did not catch it. G3's unit test ("prints 2195, sleeps, prints 2196") and 37's plain test are not affected.

**Fix.** Give `checkpoint` its own way from `n` to `m`. Do not use `hang_after_line` for that stretch.

- Deterministic option: a release file, `{"begin_after_line": n, "commit_before_line": m, "release_file": "<path>"}`. The fake waits for the file, then commits and writes line `m`. The When step sends the signal once the marker is logged. It then waits for the waiting line (on stderr in plain mode, on the terminal under the layout) and creates the file. This keeps T12 rule 5: the test waits for conditions only.
- Alternative: a pause through `line_delay_ms`, with the race named.

This is a low-impact test choice, so the design agent can pick. Then correct T4, T5 (the step and its note), R6 Tests, G3 Tests, and packages 03, 18 and 39.

### G2. The checkpoint deferral scenarios of 39b deadlock (major)

**Files:** 39 Tests, part B (R6 and G3, the checkpoint deferral as real processes, and D60's part on a pseudo-terminal). The source is 01 T4's `checkpoint` row and T5's step text, as 18 and 03 build them.
